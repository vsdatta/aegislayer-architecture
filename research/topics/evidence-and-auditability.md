# Track: Evidence and Auditability

- Research question: What evidence model best supports attributable governance review?
- Motivation: Improve reviewability and post-incident analysis.
- Scope: Event taxonomy, correlation/causation IDs, bundle finalization.
- Non-goals: Proprietary storage backends.
- Expected artifacts: Evidence lifecycle deep dive and SDK evidence bundle model.
- Evidence requirements: UTC timestamps and deterministic serialization.
- Validation approach: Evidence generation tests and walkthrough checks.
- Public/proprietary boundary: Public evidence schema concepts only.
