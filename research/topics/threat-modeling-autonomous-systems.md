# Track: Threat Modeling for Autonomous Systems

- Research question: Which threat categories most affect autonomous action safety and governance?
- Motivation: Align controls to high-value threat paths.
- Scope: Prompt abuse, authority bypass, connector abuse, evidence tampering.
- Non-goals: Zero-day exploit disclosure.
- Expected artifacts: Expanded threat-control traceability.
- Evidence requirements: Mapped control and lifecycle references.
- Validation approach: Matrix review against ADR and diagrams.
- Public/proprietary boundary: Public threat taxonomy only.
