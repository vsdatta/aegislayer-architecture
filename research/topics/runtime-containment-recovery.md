# Track: Runtime Containment and Recovery

- Research question: What containment strategies best limit blast radius while preserving accountability?
- Motivation: Improve safe response to anomalies.
- Scope: Contain/deny/resume decision models and evidence preservation.
- Non-goals: Internal incident playbook procedures.
- Expected artifacts: Incident lifecycle model and scenario walkthrough.
- Evidence requirements: Containment and decision records.
- Validation approach: Deterministic incident response simulation.
- Public/proprietary boundary: Public conceptual response model only.
