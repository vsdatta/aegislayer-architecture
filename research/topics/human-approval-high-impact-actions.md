# Track: Human Approval for High-Impact AI Actions

- Research question: What approval controls preserve accountability without blocking safe automation?
- Motivation: Balance autonomy and accountable oversight.
- Scope: Scope-bound approvals, expiry, revocation, and evidence.
- Non-goals: Org-specific workflow tooling integrations.
- Expected artifacts: Approval lifecycle and conformance criteria.
- Evidence requirements: Approval records, rationale, and timing linkage.
- Validation approach: Deterministic approval-path scenarios.
- Public/proprietary boundary: Public process patterns only.
