# Track: Identity and Authority for Agentic Systems

- Research question: What identity and authority models best constrain autonomous actions?
- Motivation: Prevent implicit trust and authority overreach.
- Scope: Claim validation, delegated authority, scope/expiry constraints.
- Non-goals: Vendor-specific IAM implementations.
- Expected artifacts: Lifecycle definitions and control criteria.
- Evidence requirements: Attributable actor and authority context evidence.
- Validation approach: Threat-to-control traceability and denial-path testing.
- Public/proprietary boundary: Public conceptual schemas only.
