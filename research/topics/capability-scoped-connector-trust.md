# Track: Capability-Scoped Connector Trust

- Research question: How can capability scoping and connector readiness prevent trust expansion?
- Motivation: Connectors are high-impact authority amplifiers.
- Scope: Capability issuance, readiness checks, and revocation signals.
- Non-goals: Proprietary connector implementations.
- Expected artifacts: Connector lifecycle and unsafe pattern examples.
- Evidence requirements: Connector state and invocation decision records.
- Validation approach: Readiness failure and revocation simulations.
- Public/proprietary boundary: Public connector descriptors only.
