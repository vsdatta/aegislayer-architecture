# Track: Policy-Before-Execution Architectures

- Research question: How should policy decisions be positioned and enforced before external effects occur?
- Motivation: Limit post-hoc policy enforcement gaps.
- Scope: Structured request normalization and deterministic policy outcomes.
- Non-goals: Proprietary policy engines.
- Expected artifacts: Decision lifecycle and policy simulator scenarios.
- Evidence requirements: Policy input/output records with reason codes.
- Validation approach: Reference SDK policy and runtime tests.
- Public/proprietary boundary: Public policy patterns and examples only.
