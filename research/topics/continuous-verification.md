# Track: Continuous Verification

- Research question: How should trust be re-evaluated during long-running execution?
- Motivation: Mitigate stale approvals, capability drift, and connector degradation.
- Scope: Monitoring triggers, policy re-evaluation, revocation behavior.
- Non-goals: Proprietary observability pipelines.
- Expected artifacts: Monitoring lifecycle and revocation simulation.
- Evidence requirements: Trigger, response, and containment logs.
- Validation approach: Runtime drift scenarios in SDK and demo.
- Public/proprietary boundary: Public control patterns only.
