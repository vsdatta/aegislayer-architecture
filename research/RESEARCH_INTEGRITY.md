# Research Integrity

Public research outputs in this repository must:

- Distinguish hypotheses, assumptions, and validated findings.
- Avoid fabricated data, experiments, certifications, or adoption claims.
- Cite sources accurately.
- Keep conceptual architecture claims bounded.
- Preserve reproducibility expectations for any future empirical work.

## Red-Line Rules

Do not publish:

- Invented benchmark outcomes
- Unverified external claims presented as fact
- Customer-specific confidential results
- Security guarantees framed as absolute prevention
