# AegisLayer Hugging Face Branding Guide

## Visual Direction

AegisLayer uses a repository-owned visual system built around deep green authority tones, warm signal accents, pale paper-like backgrounds, and serif-led headings paired with pragmatic sans-serif body copy. The visual tone should feel technical, calm, and review-oriented rather than hype-oriented.

## Usage Rules

- Keep GitHub as the canonical source link in every asset package.
- Do not imitate another organization's protected marks, mascots, or product UI.
- Do not use unlicensed photography or external logo packs.
- Keep contrast high enough for accessible reading.
- Keep motion minimal and meaningful, with reduced-motion support.

## Messaging Tone

- factual
- architecture-first
- transparent about conceptual scope
- explicit about limitations
- specific about what each Space or dataset contains

"Public reference architecture, deterministic explainers, and synthetic governance datasets for accountable AI action systems."
