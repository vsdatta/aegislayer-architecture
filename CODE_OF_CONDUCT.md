# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

AegisLayer Architecture is intended to support serious, respectful, and technically grounded collaboration around AI security, governance, and resilient autonomous systems.

Participants are expected to contribute in a way that is professional, constructive, inclusive, and safe.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- Engaging respectfully with different viewpoints
- Providing specific, evidence-based technical feedback
- Distinguishing facts from assumptions and opinions
- Acknowledging mistakes and correcting them promptly
- Protecting confidential, private, and security-sensitive information
- Focusing criticism on ideas, designs, and evidence rather than individuals
- Respecting project scope, review processes, and licensing terms

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation
- Personal attacks or deliberately inflammatory conduct
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, secrets, customer data, or exploit details publicly
- Knowingly making false or misleading claims
- Coordinated disruption, spam, or abuse of project channels
- Retaliation against people who report concerns in good faith

## Security-Sensitive Conduct

Security vulnerabilities must be handled according to `SECURITY.md`.

Do not post active exploit details, credentials, private infrastructure information, or sensitive proof-of-concept material in public issues, discussions, or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Reports should include enough context for a fair review. Deliberately false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.

## Reporting

For general conduct concerns, contact:

**<governance@aegislayer.ai>**

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, documentation, project communications, and public interactions representing the project.
