# Ethical Promotion Policy

## Commitments

- no fake engagement
- no bots
- no deceptive tagging
- no misleading titles
- no fabricated metrics or endorsements
- no mislabeling docs or datasets as models

## Posting Standard

Every public post must:

- link to a useful artifact
- describe the artifact truthfully
- disclose conceptual, synthetic, or reference status where relevant
- invite specific feedback
- avoid exaggerated claims

## Review Checklist

- Is the linked artifact public and reachable?
- Does the copy avoid unsupported claims?
- Does the post ask a real question or invite meaningful response?
- Is the message materially different from recent posts?
- Would a technically skeptical reader consider it accurate?
