# Community Engagement Guide

## Goals

- invite specific technical feedback
- keep discussions grounded in published artifacts
- make it easy for new readers to ask beginner questions
- avoid repetitive self-promotion

## Engagement Rules

- always link to one useful artifact
- ask one concrete question per post
- disclose conceptual or synthetic status clearly
- respond with clarifications, not hype
- collect recurring questions for docs improvements

## Good Engagement Patterns

- ask which threat-control mapping needs clearer rationale
- ask whether a scenario boundary is too broad or too narrow
- ask which glossary term still feels ambiguous
- ask what SDK example would be most valuable next

## Patterns To Avoid

- vague calls for follows or likes
- inflated performance or adoption claims
- repeated tagging of unrelated communities
- posting without a linked artifact or feedback hook
